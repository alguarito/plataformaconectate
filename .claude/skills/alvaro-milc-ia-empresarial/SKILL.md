# Alvaro MILC IA Empresarial

## Purpose

Use this skill for the curricular line that connects artificial intelligence with the school's business, commercial, and accounting modality.

The goal is to help students use IA with criterio: prompt design, verification, authorship, copyright, data ethics, business communication, reports, dashboards, process improvement, automation, and decision defense.

## Reference Use

Read `references/linea-ia-empresarial.md` when the task involves:

- adjusting grade 9, 10, or 11 AI/business themes;
- designing sessions, guides, projects, exams, rubrics, or final products in this line;
- checking continuity from basic AI to business application;
- connecting IA with writing, LaTeX, dashboards, reports, digital presence, automation, or pitch;
- aligning projects and evaluations with the plan area sequence already built.

## Curricular Spine

- Grade 9 period 3: basic IA, prompts, verification, bias, privacy, and responsible school use.
- Grade 10 period 1: reverse writing, free-topic book creation, ethics, copyright, authorship, editing, prompt log.
- Grade 10 period 2: from book to professional report, LaTeX, business/commercial reports, market studies, data, citations, transparency.
- Grade 10 period 3: data loading, cleaning, visualization, dashboard or assisted business report, AI-supported decision defense.
- Grade 11 period 1: business digital presence, brand identity, web architecture, SEO, commercial writing, ethical IA.
- Grade 11 period 2: business processes, forms, data organization, automation, customer service with IA, PDCA, privacy.
- Grade 11 period 3: integrated entrepreneurial project with IA, data, dashboard, automation, documentation, pitch, and MILC tribunal.

## Design Rules

- IA is an assistant, not a substitute for student authorship.
- Every IA product should include evidence of human revision.
- Ask for prompt logs when students use generative IA.
- Include verification steps for factual claims, data interpretation, and business recommendations.
- Require declaration of IA use in books, reports, dashboards, websites, and pitches.
- Treat personal data and client/user information as sensitive.
- Connect outputs to business, commercial, or accounting decisions whenever grade 10 or 11 is involved.

## Product Patterns

- **Prompt lab**: role, context, task, format, criteria, output, revision.
- **Book with IA**: theme, audience, index, chapters, prompt log, editing, credits, copyright note.
- **LaTeX report**: objective, data, table, graph, analysis, citation, IA declaration.
- **Dashboard**: dataset, cleaning, indicators, charts, findings, recommendation, risk.
- **Digital presence**: brand identity, audience, value proposition, landing page, SEO, content calendar.
- **Process automation**: process map, form, data capture, automated response, privacy protocol.
- **Pitch**: problem, evidence, solution, indicators, impact, costs, risks, sustainability.

## Assessment Guidance

Assess:

- clarity and specificity of prompts;
- quality of human revision and authorship;
- ethical use, copyright, citation, and IA transparency;
- data quality and verification;
- business reasoning and decision defense;
- product coherence and usefulness;
- reflection on risks, bias, privacy, and social impact.

For final exams, prefer applied business cases over isolated definitions.

## Output Expectations

When using this skill, report:

- the grade-period line being used;
- the IA/business product expected;
- ethical safeguards included;
- how the task supports the school modality;
- where changes should be made: temario, guide source, generator, project, exam, rubric, or plan area.
